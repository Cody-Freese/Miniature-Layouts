# Skavenblight — Western Cliffhold
## Procurement and terrain build checklist (revision 1; 8 × 8 ft)

**Scope**: terrain and scenery only; excludes the table/benchwork. All terrain quantities below are **concept-level targets**, not STL part counts or final cutting dimensions. Final quantities follow a 1:1 paper template of each STL and a scale test print. 32mm heroic miniature scale is the target for the buildings; the city itself is selectively compressed.

## Plan and elevations
- [ ] 96 × 96 in square landscape / 16 possible 24 × 24 in removable scenery modules
- [ ] Western mountain: x 0–34 in, varying footprint; summit 46–52 in high, removable upper mountain
- [ ] Northern ridges: along y 78–96 in, blending into western massif, peaks 22–38 in
- [ ] Main walled city: irregular oval, roughly x 27–92, y 17–81; outer rampart length approx. 16–19 ft including gates and buttresses
- [ ] Outer ground z +2 in, inner fractured ground z +4 in
- [ ] Second level roughly z +9 in; third level roughly z +16 in
- [ ] Bell tower near x 63, y 56; reaches z +36–40 in, independently removable
- [ ] Western machinery pit approx. 16 × 19 in; eastern forge pit approx. 14 × 22 in (verify terrace overlap)
- [ ] Southern swamp + ruined siege roads and eastern outer armory / foundry
- [ ] At least three tunnel or gnawhole entrances with removable façades

## Core structural and scenic supply categories (exclude table/benchwork)
- [ ] XPS rigid insulation panels (1–2 in thickness), starting allowance ~12–24 panels of 2 × 4 ft for a largely hollow shell; exact number depends on mountain armature and terrace design.
- [ ] Foamcore / high-density foam board for terrace fascia and hollow substructures.
- [ ] Lightweight shell armature stock (cardboard ribs, thin wood / PVC reinforcement as appropriate) for the 4-ft-high mountain; do not carve the entire mountain from solid XPS.
- [ ] Sculpting materials: lightweight spackle, foam-safe gap filler, acrylic texture paste, fine sand, small slate chips, cork bark, talus / model railroad scree, plaster cloth selectively.
- [ ] Waterproofed painted water basins, acrylic water effects or shallow pour resin in small test areas; never pour in a large recess before leak and heat tests.
- [ ] Static grass (dead yellow/brown), dead reeds, weeds, root debris, moss tufts, miniature dead trees and scrub.
- [ ] Snow flock / granular snow product for **mountain tops only**, with a transition to bare black rock and contaminated vegetation below.
- [ ] Weathering: hobby acrylic base paints (bulk dark stone gray, black, off-white, brown, rust, ochre, desaturated green), colored texture washes, dry pigments, soot, moss, matte varnish, gloss for wet slime.
- [ ] Weathered timbers (balsa / basswood / coffee stirrers), dowels, brass/plastic tubing for pipes, styrene strips and sheets, jewelry chain, wire, mesh/grating, magnet pairs, removable locating dowels, plasticard and scrap gears.
- [ ] FDM printing: PLA/PLA+ rolls (rough early material planning band 25–60 kg across a dense 8×8 city, with large uncertainty); 0.4mm nozzle, 0.20mm layers, 10–15% infill for many terrain components; test print a complete facade and wall module before estimating total mass.
- [ ] Adhesives: PVA, foam-compatible construction adhesive, cyanoacrylate plus accelerator if needed, plastic cement for GW plastic, epoxy for high-load joints. Avoid solvent products that attack XPS.
- [ ] Tools: hot-wire foam cutter (ventilate; never cut treated coatings), retractable utility knife, hobby knife, sanding tools, clamps, miniature hand drill, soldering station, safety glasses, dust mask appropriate to sanding materials, measuring tools and steel straightedge.

## Lighting, smoke, warpstone crystals and maintenance
- [ ] Select a **single low-voltage lighting system**, e.g. 12V green COB LED strips for exposed fissures and 12V warm-white/amber strips for forges; compatible 12V prewired micro-LEDs for windows and portals.
- [ ] A suitably rated, safety-certified 12V power supply with calculated load and margin, fused distribution, appropriately sized wire, accessible labeled switches, 6–10 independent zones, dimmers where appropriate.
- [ ] Frosted / sanded clear acrylic sheet, light diffuser film, translucent green acetate or casting plastic for hiding individual LEDs in pits and portals.
- [ ] 150–300 small translucent green acrylic or glass pebbles/crystal chips as a **provisional** starting scenic quantity, plus 20–40 larger crystal clusters / faceted resin casts, depending on density.
- [ ] Clear acrylic rods, light pipes or optic fibers for vertical shafts and warpstone conduits (optional).
- [ ] Loose teased cotton / polyester fiberfill for green-lit fumes and gray smoke; **LED only**, away from all hot lamps, flame, hot resistors and driver boards. Keep fiber removable for cleaning.
- [ ] Heat-shrink tubing, insulated connectors, solder and strain relief, routing raceways, removable electronic access panels, and a power-off master switch.
- [ ] Use lighting effect in **layers**: LED → diffuser → crystals / acrylic rods → teased cotton wisps. Avoid burying power supplies or connections in resin or foam.

## Color scheme
- Dark stone: charcoal black/gray base, dirty gray drybrush, green grime in lower masonry joints.
- Wood: weathered gray-brown, blackened edges, occasional reddish rot.
- Iron: near-black metal, rust orange and oxidized bronze, grime washes.
- Warp machinery: greens from near-black at unlit edges to yellow-green and pale off-white around the brightest crystals.
- Snow: dirty blue-gray shadow, stark off-white at highest peaks, soot toward factories.
- Wet areas: black-green water, turquoise-green reflections near exposed energy, gloss varnish.
- Existing Citadel paints can handle highlight and specialty applications; buy inexpensive bulk craft paint for the large-area groundwork.

## Prototyping and build sequence
1. Mask the 96 × 96 footprint on the floor; lay out full-size paper templates for western massif, ramparts, two pits, ring footprints and tower. Check reach/access before construction.
2. Print **one** rampart module, one tenement facade, one stair connection and one cathedral wall section at native 100% size, test against actual models/bases.
3. Design the 24 × 24 in removable sections and removable mountain / tower assemblies.
4. Build lightweight mountain shells, keep front-access portals and wiring chases.
5. Install the wire runs, low-voltage supply distribution and diffusers; test before covering pits or cliffs.
6. Carve outer terrain, build cliff face and terrace risers, drill vent / wiring routes.
7. Assemble wall ring, gate, exposed two undercity cuts and footpaths/ramp connections.
8. Fill neighborhoods with mixed full/ruined buildings; texture and weather.
9. Create bell housing with removable bell element, then paint and mount skyline spires.
10. Install water, dead vegetation, snow, surface crystals and fiber smoke last.
11. Test all circuits, maintenance access and sunset shadow direction.

## Sunset-light geometry (approximation)
For a west-side point light / low sun, shadow occurs if the mountain intercepts the ray to the bell. With a 50-inch peak, 40-inch bell and 45-inch horizontal separation, the bell remains shadowed for westward light altitude below approximately arctan(10/45) ≈ 12.5 degrees, **if the light and cliff align along the same west-to-east ray**. Refine after a digital light simulation and/or the silhouette prototype.

## Notes
- Do not confuse '32mm heroic' building details with a *literal* scaled whole metropolis. A capital city of thousands of inhabitants cannot fit onto a 96-inch board at 1:54; the footprint is compressed fantasy scenery.
- For actual matched-play AoS, leave removable legal terrain and a detached battle area. Most of this city is primarily a diorama, though many modules can also be used for narrative play.
- The HTML rotatable viewer depicts color-block approximations of the intended printed assets. It does **not** contain/redistribute any purchased STL models.
